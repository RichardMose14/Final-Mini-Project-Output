About Pine Hall Café

A student-run coffee counter with a simple goal: make campus feel a little more like home.

How we started

Pine Hall Café began as a Student Council pilot in a storage room with one espresso machine. Students kept asking for somewhere to study that was not the library and not their dorm.

Today we serve about 400 cups a day, and every shift is staffed by students who learn barista skills, inventory and customer service on the job.

What we care about

Fair prices

Nothing on our menu costs more than a meal at the canteen.

Less waste

Reusable cups get a discount, and used grounds go to the campus garden.

Student jobs

Every dollar of profit goes back into paid student shifts and scholarships.
